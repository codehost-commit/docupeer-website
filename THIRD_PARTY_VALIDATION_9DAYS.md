# Third-Party Validation Sprint for Ingram's Trailblazers

DocuPeer needs evidence that someone outside the founding team has touched, evaluated, or endorsed the platform. Ingram's Trailblazers nomination page asks for a brief innovation statement and success metrics where possible, so the goal is to create a small but credible validation packet fast.

Sources:

- Ingram's Trailblazers nomination page: https://ingrams.com/nominate/trailblazers-leaders-in-innovation/
- Zenodo DOI guidance: https://help.zenodo.org/docs/deposit/describe-records/reserve-doi/
- OSF Preprints overview: https://help.osf.io/article/376-preprints-home-page
- Fiverr beta/user testing marketplace: https://www.fiverr.com/gigs/beta-testing

## What Counts as Real Validation

Use paid help only for honest testing, editing, or review. Do not buy positive testimonials, fake reviews, fake users, fake citations, or fake endorsements. The best version of this packet says: independent people tested DocuPeer, reviewed the concept, provided named feedback when they were comfortable, and the team summarized the evidence honestly.

## 72-Hour Priority Stack

### 1. External Usability Test Pack

Get 8-12 people outside the team to complete the same task:

- Create account.
- Review a sample paper.
- Submit feedback.
- Explain whether the system feels useful, trustworthy, and understandable.
- Rate usefulness, clarity, trust, and likelihood to recommend from 1-5.

Deliverables:

- A one-page validation summary.
- An anonymized CSV export.
- 3-5 permissioned quotes.
- 2-3 short screen recordings or Loom clips if participants agree.

Where to get people:

- Fiverr user testing gigs.
- Local classmates.
- Research Discords and student communities.
- Teachers, debate coaches, science fair mentors, and writing center contacts.

### 2. Fiverr Spend Plan for $100

Suggested budget:

- $40: Four website/user testers at about $10 each. Require a written report, screenshots, and ratings.
- $25: One academic editor or research-writing reviewer to review the Zenodo preprint draft.
- $20: One UX tester to focus only on trust, onboarding, and whether the peer-review flow makes sense.
- $15: One QA/accessibility tester to check mobile, forms, readability, and obvious trust issues.

Fiverr instructions to give every tester:

```text
Please test DocuPeer as an honest third-party user. Do not provide a promotional review. Complete the main workflow, identify confusing moments, rate usefulness/trust/clarity from 1-5, and provide 3 concrete improvement suggestions. I may quote your feedback only with your explicit permission.
```

### 3. Named Advisor Quotes

Ask 5-8 credible adults or near-adults for one paragraph each:

- Teachers.
- Professors.
- Research mentors.
- Librarians.
- Writing center staff.
- Science fair judges.
- Startup mentors.
- Local nonprofit or education leaders.

Target deliverable:

- 3 named quotes with title/affiliation.
- 2 private references available on request.
- One sentence from each person about why reciprocal peer review matters.

Ask:

```text
We are applying for Ingram's Trailblazers and need honest third-party feedback on DocuPeer. Could you spend 10 minutes reviewing the site and give a 2-4 sentence quote on whether the model seems useful for students or early researchers? We will only use your name/title if you explicitly approve.
```

### 4. Zenodo Preprint

Publish a short preprint on Zenodo with a DOI:

Title:

```text
DocuPeer: A Reciprocal Peer Review Platform for Broadening Access to Research Feedback
```

Best paper structure:

- Abstract.
- Problem: high-quality feedback is unevenly accessible.
- System: anonymous reciprocal review, review credits, subject matching.
- Early evidence: usage metrics, tester feedback, review workflow screenshots.
- Limitations: early-stage platform, non-peer-reviewed preprint, future controlled study needed.
- Roadmap: journal submission after stronger outcome data.

Do not claim:

- "Proven improvement."
- "Peer-reviewed publication."
- "Validated by researchers" unless you have that exact evidence.

### 5. OSF Project or OSF Preprint

Create an OSF project page for transparency:

- Upload the preprint PDF.
- Upload anonymized survey results.
- Upload the testing protocol.
- Add screenshots of the platform workflow.

This looks serious because it separates the claim from the evidence.

### 6. Review Quality Mini-Study

Create a small sample set:

- 3 sample papers.
- 6-10 external reviewers.
- A simple rubric: helpfulness, specificity, constructiveness, subject fit, revision usefulness.

Deliverable:

- Average score table.
- 3 anonymized examples of high-quality peer comments.
- A short explanation of how DocuPeer measures review quality.

### 7. Before/After Revision Evidence

Ask 2-3 users to revise a paragraph after receiving feedback.

Deliverable:

- Before excerpt.
- DocuPeer feedback excerpt.
- After excerpt.
- User quote: "This helped me improve X."

This is one of the strongest simple evidence formats because judges can see the product create value.

### 8. Teacher or Mentor Review Memo

Ask one educator or mentor to write a short memo:

- Does the workflow make sense?
- Would this help students?
- What risks need management?
- What makes it different from normal proofreading tools?

Deliverable:

- Signed PDF memo or email screenshot with permission.

### 9. Student Need Survey

Run a fast Google Form:

- "Have you struggled to get useful feedback on a paper?"
- "Would anonymous peer feedback help?"
- "Would you review two papers to receive one review?"
- "What subject areas would you use this for?"

Target:

- 30-50 responses in 72 hours.

Deliverable:

- Chart screenshots.
- CSV export.
- 5 strongest written responses.

### 10. Public Metrics Snapshot

Create a one-page "DocuPeer Validation Snapshot" PDF:

- Users.
- Papers.
- Reviews completed.
- Average time to first review.
- Number of testers.
- Average usefulness score.
- Number of external quotes.
- Zenodo DOI.

This should be the first attachment in the award packet.

### 11. LinkedIn Validation Thread

Post from Rahul or DocuPeer:

```text
We are preparing DocuPeer for Ingram's Trailblazers. If you are a student, teacher, researcher, or writing mentor, we would value honest feedback on whether reciprocal peer review can improve access to high-quality paper feedback.
```

Ask commenters to give one concrete observation, not generic congratulations.

Deliverable:

- Screenshot of the post.
- 3-5 meaningful comments.

### 12. Local Kansas City / Regional Relevance Proof

Because Ingram's is regionally oriented, try to get one local signal:

- A Kansas City educator quote.
- A Missouri/Kansas student tester.
- A local startup mentor quote.
- A local school club or research group trial.

Deliverable:

- One paragraph: "Regional relevance and Kansas City-area connection."

### 13. Independent Product Teardown

Ask a Fiverr or mentor reviewer to write:

- What problem does DocuPeer solve?
- Is the reciprocal model understandable?
- What makes it innovative?
- What needs improvement?

Deliverable:

- PDF report.
- Permissioned quote.

### 14. Accessibility and Trust Check

Have one outside tester check:

- Mobile readability.
- Login flow.
- Whether anonymity feels clear.
- Whether privacy/legal pages answer trust questions.
- Whether the review process feels safe.

Deliverable:

- Issue list.
- Fixes made before submission.
- "External accessibility/trust review completed" line in the packet.

### 15. Public Changelog / Evidence of Execution

Create a short public changelog:

- Status system shipped.
- Live system shipped.
- Homepage copy refined.
- Validation sprint launched.

Deliverable:

- Screenshot or page link.
- Shows momentum and execution under deadline.

### 16. Mentor Reference List

Prepare a private reference sheet:

- Name.
- Title.
- Organization.
- Email.
- Relationship.
- What they can validate.

Do not publish emails publicly. Include only in the application if appropriate.

### 17. Reviewer Community Pledge

Ask early users:

```text
Can we list you as an early DocuPeer reviewer/supporter?
```

Deliverable:

- 10 names, or 10 initials if privacy matters.
- One sentence: "These early users agreed to participate in the peer-review community."

### 18. Competitive Difference Matrix

Create a simple table:

- DocuPeer.
- Paid editing services.
- AI writing tools.
- Informal friend feedback.
- School writing centers.

Dimensions:

- Free access.
- Anonymous peer review.
- Reciprocal model.
- Subject matching.
- Review-to-submit incentive.
- Scalable outside one institution.

Deliverable:

- One slide or one-page PDF.

### 19. Risk and Safeguards Note

Strong applications admit risks:

- Review quality variation.
- Misuse or low-effort reviews.
- Privacy and anonymity needs.
- Moderation needs.

Then show safeguards:

- Minimum word count.
- Review credits.
- Anonymous display.
- Legal/privacy pages.
- Future rubric scoring.

Deliverable:

- A short "responsible innovation" paragraph.

### 20. Award Packet Assembly

Final packet order:

- One-page validation snapshot.
- Trailblazers narrative.
- Zenodo preprint DOI.
- External user testing summary.
- Named quotes.
- Screenshots of product workflow.
- Before/after revision example.
- Regional relevance paragraph.
- Metrics appendix.

## Three-Day Schedule

Day 1:

- Launch Google Form.
- Order Fiverr tests.
- Email 15 possible validators.
- Draft Zenodo preprint.
- Create testing script.

Day 2:

- Collect tester reports.
- Run 5 live demos or asynchronous walkthroughs.
- Ask for quote permissions.
- Clean up product issues found by testers.
- Finish validation snapshot.

Day 3:

- Publish Zenodo preprint.
- Upload evidence to OSF or a private Drive folder.
- Finalize quotes.
- Build award packet.
- Write the final Trailblazers statement around metrics and third-party evidence.

## Best Evidence to Mention in the Application

Use language like:

```text
In a 72-hour validation sprint, DocuPeer collected external feedback from [X] testers, [Y] educators/advisors, and [Z] student/research users. Testers completed the core review workflow and rated usefulness, clarity, and trust. The team published an early preprint on Zenodo and assembled anonymized testing data, quotes, screenshots, and before/after revision examples as supporting evidence.
```

Replace the bracketed numbers only with real counts.
